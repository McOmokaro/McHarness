# The Pain Extractor

A niche is not a market. A market is a **pain that gets worse over time** plus a **person who will pay to stop the worsening**. This skill finds that pain and turns it into a transformation.

## The core formula

> **Pain × Time = Worsening Pain**

Two things must both be true:

1. **There is pain** — a felt, current, expensive problem.
2. **It is worsening** — it gets *worse* the longer it goes unfixed. Time is on the pain's side, not the buyer's.

Pain alone is a one-time sale. **Worsening** pain is a business: the buyer must act eventually, and every month of delay costs them more. That urgency is what carries a high-ticket price.

If the pain is stable or shrinking, it's a maintenance product, not a movement. If it's worsening, you're selling relief from a clock.

## The two channels: Health and Wealth

Every worsening pain that reliably converts runs on **one of two channels** (sometimes both). Test each niche against these:

- **Health** — the body, energy, sleep, longevity, mobility, cognitive decline, mental load, stress, safety. The buyer fears losing capability they currently have.
- **Wealth** — money in, money out, time-cost, risk of loss, status, security, opportunity cost. The buyer fears losing money they have or missing money they could have.

If a niche's pain is neither Health nor Wealth, it usually won't pay high-ticket — it's preference, entertainment, or vanity. Park it.

Ask of the niche:
- What is the Health cost of doing nothing? (Gets sicker? Slower? Degrades?)
- What is the Wealth cost of doing nothing? (Loses revenue? Pays more each year? Falls behind competitors?)
- Which channel is **worsening faster**? Lead with that one.

## Extract the transformation: Point A → Point B

Do not sell the *fix*. Sell the *difference*. Write two states:

- **Point A (now, and getting worse):** the buyer's concrete, specific present. What it costs *today*, and where it lands in 12 months if untouched. Use their words.
- **Point B (after):** the same buyer, the same life, with the worsening stopped and reversed. Concrete, owned, believable. Not a fantasy — a *state they can recognise as theirs*.

The offer lives in the **gap** between A and B, and the price is anchored to the cost of staying at A.

Template:

```
NICHE:            <who>
CHANNEL:          Health | Wealth | both
PAIN:             <the felt problem, in their words>
WORSENING RATE:   <what gets worse per week/month, and by how much>
POINT A:          <where they are now → where they are in 12 months untouched>
POINT B:          <the same person, worsening stopped and reversed>
GAP / OFFER:      <what actually moves A→B>
PRICE ANCHOR:     <the cost of staying at A — this justifies the ticket>
URGENCY:          <the clock that forces a decision>
```

## Rules

- **Be specific or be ignored.** "Save time" is dead. "Stop losing the 11pm hour that keeps you from your kids" lives.
- **Time must make it worse.** If waiting is harmless, there's no urgency and no high-ticket price.
- **One channel leads.** Pick the faster-worsening channel; layer the other only if it genuinely fits.
- **The offer is the gap, not the tool.** People don't buy the wrench; they buy the leak stopped.
- **Price to the pain, not the effort.** Anchor to the cost of Point A, not hours worked.

## What this produces

A one-page extract: the niche, the channel, the worsening pain in the buyer's own language, and the A→B transformation with a price anchor — ready to become a landing page, a cold-email angle, or a high-ticket offer.
